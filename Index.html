Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our phone tracking service

Is phone number tracking legal?

Yes, our service is completely legal when used for legitimate purposes such as identifying unknown callers or verifying business contacts. We comply with all privacy laws and regulations and only provide information that is publicly available or obtained through legal means.

How accurate is the location data?

Our location data is highly accurate, typically providing location information within a few hundred meters for mobile numbers. For landline numbers, we can pinpoint the registered address. Accuracy may vary depending on the carrier, available data, and whether the number is mobile or landline.

Can I track any phone number in the world?

Yes, our service works with phone numbers from most countries worldwide. However, the amount of available information may vary by country and carrier due to different regulations and data availability. We currently support tracking in 190+ countries with continuous expansion.

Is my search history stored?

No, we value your privacy. We do not store any personal information or search history. All tracking requests are processed anonymously, and we have a strict no-logs policy to ensure your searches remain completely private and confidential.
